Supported Barcode Formats

  • CODE128: The most versatile format. Supports both numbers and text characters. Great for internal tracking.
  • EAN-13: The standard barcode used globally for retail products. Requires exactly 12 or 13 digits.
  • UPC: Universally accepted in the US and Canada for point-of-sale scanning.
  • CODE39: An older alphanumeric format still widely used in automotive and healthcare industries.
