Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Tveezal Tools handles information when you visit our website and use our online tools.

1. Overview

Welcome to Tveezal Tools. We provide browser-based utilities designed to help users perform common tasks involving images, PDFs, QR codes, text, calculations, conversions and other digital content.

We aim to provide useful tools without requiring an account for ordinary use. Many tools are designed to process selected content directly inside your web browser.

Important: The technical behavior of individual tools may differ. A tool page may use a third-party library, API or remote service when that is necessary for its functionality. Please read any tool-specific information provided on the relevant page before using it with sensitive material.

2. Information We Collect

Tveezal Tools does not require users to create an account simply to use ordinary no-login tools. Depending on how the website and its supporting services are configured, limited information may nevertheless be processed.

Information you voluntarily provide

  • Your name, email address or other details if you contact us.
  • The contents of a support or feedback message you voluntarily submit.
  • Information you enter into a feature when the feature needs it to operate.

Technical information

Website infrastructure, security providers, analytics systems or advertising providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location, referring page and access time.

Some technical information may be collected directly by third-party service providers rather than by Tveezal Tools.

3. Files & Client-Side Processing

Many Tveezal Tools utilities are intended to perform processing locally in your browser. For example, an image compression tool can read an image selected from your device, process it using browser APIs and generate a new file without requiring the original file to be uploaded to a Tveezal Tools server.

When a tool operates entirely client-side, the file may remain within your browser's memory during processing. Temporary data can normally disappear when the page is closed or the browser releases the relevant resources.

Important: Client-side processing should not be assumed for every current or future feature. If a tool requires server-side processing or an external API, that tool may transmit data to the service required to perform the operation.

For confidential, private or highly sensitive documents, always check the individual tool's description and processing behavior before uploading or selecting a file.

4. How Information Is Used

Information that is available to us or to relevant service providers may be used for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Providing and maintaining website functionality.
  • Processing support and contact requests.
  • Detecting abuse, fraud, malicious activity or security threats.
  • Monitoring reliability, performance and technical errors.
  • Understanding how visitors use the website when analytics are enabled.
  • Displaying, measuring or improving advertisements when advertising services are enabled.
  • Improving the usability, quality and performance of Tveezal Tools.

We do not require an account database for ordinary no-login tool usage.

5. Cookies & Browser Storage

Tveezal Tools may use browser technologies such as cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB when required for preferences, temporary tool state, functionality, security, analytics or advertising.

Local browser storage

Some preferences or temporary information may be stored on your own device. You can normally remove this information by clearing the relevant browser data.

Cookies

Cookies or similar technologies may be used by Tveezal Tools or by third-party providers. Advertising and analytics providers may use their own technologies under their respective privacy policies.

6. Advertising

Tveezal Tools may display advertisements to help support the development, hosting and maintenance of the website and its free tools.

Depending on the advertising provider and applicable settings, advertisements may involve cookies, device information, approximate location, advertising identifiers or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, frequency control or personalization.

Advertising formats and providers may change over time. We aim to place advertisements in a way that minimizes unnecessary disruption to normal tool usage.

7. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party libraries, content delivery networks, fonts, hosting infrastructure, analytics services, advertising services, security services or APIs.

When your browser communicates directly with a third-party provider, that provider may receive technical information associated with the request. Third parties generally operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Examples of third-party functionality may include:

  • JavaScript libraries required to run browser tools.
  • Font or asset delivery services.
  • Advertising and measurement platforms.
  • Analytics or performance monitoring services.
  • External APIs used by specific tools.

8. External Links

Tveezal Tools may provide links to external websites, search engines, social platforms or other services. These websites are not controlled by Tveezal Tools.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content or policies of external websites. Before providing personal information to an external service, review its privacy policy and terms.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the website's operation to help protect information and maintain service security.

However, no website, browser, network, storage system or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also keep their device, browser and operating system updated and protected.

If you believe you have discovered a security issue affecting Tveezal Tools, please contact us through the Contact page.

10. Children's Privacy

Tveezal Tools is a general-purpose online utility website. We do not intentionally design the standard tool experience to require children to create accounts or submit personal profiles.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted through a contact or other feature by a child and you have a legitimate concern about it, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

11. Your Choices & Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have privacy rights relating to personal information, such as the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or information about processing.

You may also have choices regarding cookies, personalized advertising and analytics. The controls available to you can depend on your location and on which services are active on the website.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details available on our Contact page. We may need enough information to understand and respond to a legitimate request.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, tools, data practices, advertising systems, analytics, third-party services or legal obligations change.

When changes are made, we will update the “Last updated” date shown at the top of this page. We recommend checking this page periodically for the latest version.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Tveezal Tools, please visit our Contact page.
