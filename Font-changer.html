Frequently Asked Questions

Are these real fonts or symbols?

These are special characters from the universal Unicode Standard. Because Unicode is supported by modern operating systems, web browsers, and apps, these symbols appear as formatted fonts everywhere you paste them.

Why do some devices show boxes (question marks) instead of fonts?

A few very old devices or outdated operating systems may lack support for newer Unicode glyphs. However, over 99% of modern smartphones and computers render these fonts perfectly.

Is this font changer free to use for commercial projects?

Yes. Unicode text characters are universally open and free to use in social media posts, branding drafts, YouTube titles, and personal messages.
