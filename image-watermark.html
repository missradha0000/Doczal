Add a Watermark to Images Online with InstaPic

InstaPic Image Watermark Adder is a browser-based tool for adding customizable text and logo watermarks to images. It is useful for photographers, creators, businesses, designers, social media users and anyone who wants to identify or brand their photos before sharing them online.

Upload an image, choose a text watermark or logo watermark, adjust the watermark size and opacity, select its position, rotate it when needed and export the finished image as JPG, PNG or WebP. The tool provides a live preview so you can see how the watermark will look before downloading the result.

Text Watermark for Photos

Add copyright notices, brand names, usernames, website names, business names, creator signatures or any other short text. You can change font size, weight, color, transparency, position and rotation.

Logo Watermark for Images

Use a logo or another image as a watermark. Transparent PNG logos are especially useful because the original photo remains visible underneath the watermark. Resize the logo and control its opacity to create a subtle or prominent branding effect.

Watermark Position and Rotation

Choose from common positions including top-left, top-center, top-right, center, center-left, center-right, bottom-left, bottom-center and bottom-right. Rotation can be adjusted from -180 to 180 degrees.

Repeated Watermark Pattern

For stronger protection, enable the repeated watermark option. The watermark is tiled across the image with adjustable spacing. This can make it harder to remove a watermark from a photo while still allowing the underlying image to remain visible.

Watermark Opacity

Opacity controls how transparent the watermark appears. A lower opacity creates a subtle watermark, while a higher opacity creates stronger branding or protection. For many photographs, a medium opacity is a good starting point.

JPG, PNG and WebP Output

Export the finished image as JPG for common photographic use, PNG when lossless output or transparency-related workflows are important, or WebP when you want a modern web-friendly image format supported by current browsers.

How to Add a Watermark to an Image

  1. Click Browse and select your image.
  2. Choose Text Watermark or Logo / Image.
  3. Enter your watermark text or upload your logo.
  4. Adjust size, color, opacity and rotation.
  5. Select a position or enable repeated watermark mode.
  6. Choose JPG, PNG or WebP output.
  7. Click Add Watermark & Prepare Download.
  8. Download the finished watermarked image.

Is InstaPic Watermark Adder Private?

The image processing in this tool is performed locally in the browser with JavaScript and the HTML Canvas API. No account or dedicated image-processing server is needed for the normal watermarking workflow. This design is useful when you want to add a watermark without sending the original photo to an online image-processing service.

Why Add a Watermark?

  • Show ownership or copyright information.
  • Brand photos with a company or creator logo.
  • Add a photographer name or social media handle.
  • Discourage unauthorized reuse of images.
  • Prepare branded images for social media and marketing.
  • Create preview images for clients or portfolios.

Tips for a Professional Watermark

Keep the watermark readable but avoid unnecessarily covering important subjects. Use an appropriate opacity and size. For branding, a clean logo in a corner often works well. For stronger image protection, a repeated diagonal pattern can provide broader coverage.
