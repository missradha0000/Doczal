What is Tveezal Online Image Compressor?

The Tveezal Online Image Compressor is a free, privacy-first photo optimization utility engineered to reduce image file sizes in KB and MB without degrading visible sharpness or resolution. Whether you are prepping banners for a fast-loading website, uploading government application forms with 50 KB limits, or optimizing photos for email attachments, this tool accomplishes lossless compression in milliseconds directly inside your web browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will compressing my image reduce its dimensions?

No. The pixel dimensions (width and height) remain identical. The tool optimizes internal byte allocation and color palettes to reduce file size without shrinking image bounds.

Can I compress transparent PNG files?

Yes. The tool preserves alpha channel transparency while reducing overall file size.

Is there any daily limit on image compression?

No! Because processing takes place on your own device, you can compress as many images as you want with zero fees and no registration.
