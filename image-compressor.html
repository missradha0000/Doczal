Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the photo color remain unchanged?

We use high-fidelity HTML5 Canvas rendering pipelines with native sRGB color matrices, preventing gamma shifts or faded color palettes during compression.

Can I compress to less than 50 KB (e.g., 20 KB)?

Yes. Simply type 20 in the "Target File Size (KB)" box and click "Apply to All". The engine will optimize the image to meet your constraint.

Are my uploaded photos safe?

Yes, 100%. All processing runs locally inside your browser memory. Nothing is sent to remote cloud servers or saved in databases.
