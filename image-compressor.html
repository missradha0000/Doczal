Frequently Asked Questions

Why was my first image showing the size of my third image earlier?

Standard browser event loops load images at different speeds depending on resolution, which caused previous asynchronous queues to cross-wire. Our updated pipeline assigns an immutable session UID to each file, ensuring 100% accurate sequential isolation.

Can I compress photos to 20 KB for government exam signatures?

Yes. Simply type 20 in the target box on that specific card and click "Set". The adaptive engine will compress the file down to 20 KB (±1 KB) immediately.

Does this tool work on iPhone and Android mobile browsers?

Yes. The tool is fully responsive and supports touch drag-and-drop, camera uploads, and native Android/iOS share sheets.
