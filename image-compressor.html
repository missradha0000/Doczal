Frequently Asked Questions

Why do other compressors stop at 198 KB when I asked for 50 KB?

Most basic tools only adjust a single quality factor once. If that is insufficient, they give up. Tveezal Tools uses an iterative binary search algorithm that repeatedly recalculates and adapts quality and pixel dimensions until the file matches your exact KB requirement.

What happens if my original image is already smaller than 50 KB?

If an image is already smaller than your requested target, our smart algorithm prevents artificial bloating or unnecessary re-encoding, preserving your photo's original clarity.
