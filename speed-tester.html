Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good download speed for streaming in 4K?

Netflix and YouTube recommend a minimum sustained download speed of 25 Mbps per stream for Ultra HD 4K content.

Why does speed differ between Wi-Fi and mobile data?

Wi-Fi signals can degrade due to physical walls and distance from the router, while 4G/5G mobile speeds depend on cell tower traffic and signal strength.

Is this speed test tool free?

Yes, 100% free with unlimited tests and zero account registration required.
