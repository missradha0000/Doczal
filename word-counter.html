Frequently Asked Questions

Does this word counter support languages other than English?

Yes. The tool features full Unicode UTF-8 compatibility and accurately counts words, whitespace, and characters in Hindi, Urdu, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, and many other languages.

How is the reading time calculated?

We calculate reading duration based on the universally accepted speed of 200 words per minute (WPM). If your text has 400 words, it will display a reading time of 2 minutes.

Is there a text or character limit in this tool?

No, there are no artificial limits. You can paste full book chapters or lengthy research papers smoothly without crashes.
