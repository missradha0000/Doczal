1. General Platform, Privacy & Accounts (Q1 – Q10)
Q1: What is Tveezal Tools and what makes it completely unique?
Tveezal Tools is an online ecosystem unifying over 34+ free utilities across PDF editing, batch image compression, text synthesis, and calculators. Unlike competitor sites that upload your private documents to third-party cloud servers, our architecture runs entirely inside your browser's local sandbox memory.
Q2: Do I need to create an account, register, or enter a credit card?
No. Tveezal Tools is 100% free and open. There are no registration forms, no login barriers, no subscriptions, and no hidden credits or paywalls.
Q3: Are my uploaded documents or photos saved or monitored on your servers?
Never. We utilize modern WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and client-side FileReader APIs. Your files stay strictly on your device's memory. When you refresh or close the tab, all buffers are instantly wiped clean.
Q4: Does Tveezal Tools work without an active internet connection?
Once a specific tool page (like Image Compressor or Standard Calculator) is loaded into your browser cache, its core algorithms execute locally even if your internet connection temporarily drops.
Q5: Are there daily limits on how many times I can use a tool?
No. There are zero daily usage caps. You can compress thousands of images, generate endless QR codes, or convert unlimited units at your own pace.
Q6: Which operating systems and web browsers are supported?
Tveezal Tools is universally compatible with modern web browsers including Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Opera, and Brave on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux.
Q7: Can I use Tveezal Tools on smartphones and tablets?
Yes. All interfaces are responsive, mobile-touch optimized, and support camera photo uploads, touch drag-and-drop, and native WhatsApp/Telegram sharing sheets.
Q8: Does Tveezal Tools inject watermarks onto my converted documents or pictures?
No. All generated images, merged PDFs, and audio recordings are completely clean and free of watermarks or promotional logos.
Q9: How fast is file processing on the platform?
Because there is no upload queue or server round-trip latency, file processing is virtually instant, taking only milliseconds to a couple seconds depending on your device's hardware.
Q10: How do I report a bug or suggest a new tool?
You can visit our Contact Page to submit bug reports, performance feedback, or request new tools to be added to our pipeline.

2. PDF Tools, Security & Manipulation (Q11 – Q20)
Q11: How do I combine multiple PDF files into one?
Use our PDF Merge Tool. Upload your PDF files, drag and drop them to organize their sequence, and click "Merge PDFs" to download a unified document.
Q12: Can I split specific page ranges from a large PDF document?
Yes. With our PDF Split Tool, you can extract individual pages, split by custom ranges (e.g., pages 1-4, 8, 12-15), or divide every page into independent PDFs.
Q13: How does the PDF Lock tool password-protect documents?
The PDF Lock Tool applies standard 128-bit/256-bit AES encryption to the PDF dictionary, requiring a password to open and view the content across all PDF readers.
Q14: What is the difference between PDF Protector and PDF Lock?
PDF Lock restricts viewing completely behind a user password. Our PDF Protector lets users view the document but disables permissions like printing, copying text, or form editing.
Q15: Can I remove a password from a protected PDF?
Yes. Using our PDF Unlock Tool, provide the valid authorization password once, and the tool will permanently decrypt and export a password-free copy.
Q16: How do I convert PDF pages to JPG or PNG images?
Our PDF to Image Converter renders every page onto an ultra-sharp canvas, allowing you to download high-resolution JPG or PNG images individually or as a ZIP.
Q17: Can I extract embedded images from a PDF without converting whole pages?
Yes. Use our PDF Image Extractor to extract only the embedded photos and vector illustrations without the surrounding text.
Q18: Can I add a confidential watermark or company stamp to my PDF?
Yes. The PDF Watermark Tool allows you to overlay custom text or transparent company logos with full control over rotation, opacity, and positioning.
Q19: How do I rotate or delete upside-down pages in a PDF?
Open the PDF Page Editor. It displays a thumbnail gallery of all pages where you can rotate single pages 90°/180° or delete unwanted pages with one click.
Q20: How do I change the order of pages inside a PDF?
Use the PDF Page Rearrange Tool. Simply drag page cards into your preferred sequence and export the newly organized document.

3. Image Tools, Compression & Exact KB (Q21 – Q30)
Q21: How does Tveezal Image Compressor achieve exact target sizes like 50 KB?
Our Image Compressor uses an iterative adaptive binary search engine that repeatedly tests compression matrices until the output file size matches your exact target within ±1-2 KB.
Q22: Why don't colors fade or distort during aggressive compression?
We enforce strict sRGB color profile retention and high-fidelity bilinear canvas resampling, preventing gamma shifting, pixel washing, or unnatural hue changes.
Q23: Can I compress up to 10 images at once?
Yes. You can select up to 10 photos simultaneously. The tool will process them in a sequenced pipeline without cross-swapping files between cards.
Q24: Can I set different target KB sizes for individual photos in a batch?
Yes. Each image card has its own dedicated "Target KB" input box, allowing you to compress Photo #1 to 20 KB and Photo #2 to 100 KB independently.
Q25: How do I share compressed photos directly to WhatsApp or Telegram?
Every image card features an instant "Share" button that invokes your phone or desktop's native Web Share API to send files directly without saving them first.
Q26: Which image file formats can I convert?
Using our Format Converter, you can convert between JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and ICO formats instantly.
Q27: How do I resize photos by dimensions instead of file size?
Use the Image Resizer Tool to set custom width and height in pixels, percentages, or centimeters with an optional aspect ratio lock.
Q28: How does the Background Remover work?
Our Background Remover uses an in-browser machine learning model to isolate foreground subjects and export clean, transparent PNGs.
Q29: What is Image to Base64 and why do developers need it?
The Image to Base64 Tool converts photos into ASCII string URIs, enabling web developers to embed icons directly into HTML and CSS without extra HTTP requests.
Q30: Can I watermark or remove timestamps from images?
Yes. Use Add Watermark to brand your images, or use Watermark Remover to clean unwanted stamps using smart content blending.

4. Text Analysis, Speech Synthesis & Fonts (Q31 – Q40)
Q31: How does the Word Counter calculate reading time?
Our Word Counter uses the international benchmark of 200 words per minute (WPM) to estimate how long an article or speech will take to read.
Q32: Does the Line Counter differentiate between text lines and empty lines?
Yes. The Line Counter categorizes your text into Total Lines, Non-Empty Lines, and Blank Lines, and includes a one-click button to strip out empty rows.
Q33: How many languages does the Text to Speech tool support?
Our Text to Speech Tool supports over 35+ languages, including Hindi, Punjabi, Marathi, Urdu, Tamil, Telugu, Spanish, Arabic, French, and Japanese.
Q34: Can I download generated speech audio as an audio file?
Yes. Clicking "Download Audio" records the voice synthesis stream directly to your device as an uncompressed, high-fidelity WAV file.
Q35: Are there realistic male, female, and child voices available?
Yes. Tveezal TTS includes dedicated acoustic presets for Deep Men, Natural Women, Innocent Child, Youth Girl, and Cyber Robot.
Q36: How does the Online Font Changer work?
Our Font Changer converts standard alphanumeric text into decorative Unicode glyphs (such as Gothic, Cursive, and Bubble text) that can be pasted directly into Instagram, Discord, and WhatsApp bios.
Q37: Can I repeat a message 10,000 times for messaging fun?
Yes. With our Text Repeater, enter any text or emoji, choose a repetition count up to 10,000, pick a separator (new line, space, or comma), and copy the output with one click.
Q38: Can I convert text between UPPERCASE, lowercase, and Title Case?
Yes. Our text case options convert full paragraphs to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, or Sentence Case in real time.
Q39: Does the Word Counter support non-English scripts like Hindi or Arabic?
Yes. The counter is built with full UTF-8 Unicode parsing and accurately calculates word, sentence, and character counts across non-Latin alphabets.
Q40: Is my text data saved when I use text utilities?
No. Your articles, drafts, and passwords are never transmitted over network calls; all text analysis happens in browser memory.

5. Web Security, Network, QR & Calculators (Q41 – Q50)
Q41: How does the Internet Speed Test calculate network speed?
Our Speed Tester transmits packet streams to edge CDN nodes, measuring sustained download throughput, upload capacity, round-trip ping latency, and jitter in Mbps.
Q42: Does the speed test display my real public IP address?
Yes. When you initiate the test, secure client queries identify your publicly visible IPv4/IPv6 address and display it alongside your speed results.
Q43: How does the Password Strength Tester evaluate security?
Our Password Strength Tester evaluates character entropy, checking for combinations of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols, and calculates an estimated brute-force crack time.
Q44: What types of QR codes can I create with the QR Generator?
With our All-in-One QR Generator, you can create customized QR codes for website URLs, plain text, Wi-Fi credentials, phone numbers, and vCard contact details with custom colors and logo embedding.
Q45: Can I scan QR codes and barcodes directly using my device camera?
Yes. Both our QR Code Scanner and Barcode Scanner allow you to scan codes live through your webcam/phone camera or by uploading saved image files.
Q46: Which barcode formats can I generate?
Our Barcode Generator supports retail and warehouse formats including CODE128, EAN-13, UPC-A, and CODE39.
Q47: How does the Age Calculator calculate precise age?
The Age Calculator analyzes your birthdate against the current calendar day, outputting your exact age in years, months, days, total hours, and a countdown to your next birthday.
Q48: Can I calculate loan EMIs and total interest payable?
Yes. Use our Loan / Interest Calculator to configure principal loan amounts, interest rates, and tenures to calculate monthly EMIs and total repayment costs.
Q49: Which unit conversions are supported on the platform?
We offer dedicated conversion utilities including the Length Converter, Weight Converter, and Area Converter (covering Acres, Hectares, Square Feet, and regional Bigha units).
Q50: Can I request API access or white-label tools for my website?
All tools on Tveezal Tools are designed for direct web browser usage. For business inquiries, partnership requests, or custom implementations, reach out via our Contact Us page.
